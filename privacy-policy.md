# Church Recorder Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 16, 2026

This policy covers Church Recorder for Android, an independent app for listening to public church streams and saving recordings on your device. It is not the official Listen to Church app.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores your selected churches, recording preferences, appearance settings, church time-zone/service-time settings, and recordings in its private storage. No Church Recorder account is required. The app has no advertising or analytics SDK. The app does not upload your recordings or send a list of your selections to the developer.

## Network connections and third parties

To check stream status and play or record audio, the app connects directly to Listen to Church and its audio relay servers. The requested URL identifies the stream you chose. Like other internet services, those servers necessarily receive your IP address and request information needed to provide the connection and may process it under their own policies. Church Recorder does not control those services or their logs. Consult Listen to Church at https://listentochurch.com for its current policies.

The public-stream client does not read or send personal Listen to Church account cookies. A church controls whether its audio is public, restricted, available, or offline.

The Google Play edition receives app updates through Google Play and does not download or install APK updates from GitHub. The separate direct-download preview edition may connect to GitHub to check and download its updates. Opening this policy or another external link uses your browser and the destination's own privacy practices.

## Recording, playback, and device permissions

The app uses internet/network access for streams, foreground services and wake locks for user-enabled monitoring/recording and playback, and notifications for recording/playback controls. It records network audio, not your microphone. Background monitoring continues only when enabled and can be stopped in the app or its notification.

Recordings and app preferences remain local unless you choose to export them. Android's file picker lets you choose where an exported file is saved; if you choose a cloud storage provider, that provider handles the file under its policies. The app does not automatically send recordings to the developer.

## Retention and deletion

Unsaved app recordings become eligible for automatic deletion 30 days after they end. Existing recordings receive a fresh 30-day grace period when the retention feature first handles them. Successful exports to Downloads mark the library copy as kept. Active recordings and current playback are protected; Android may defer maintenance. Exported files outside the app are not deleted by its cleanup feature.

You can delete recordings from the app. Uninstalling removes the app's private local data. Files already exported elsewhere must be deleted from that destination separately.

## Security

The app's supported network stream connections use HTTPS. Private app storage and Android's app sandbox protect local data. No app can guarantee complete security, and you should protect access to your device and exported recordings.

## Children

The app does not require users to provide a name, email address, or account, and has no chat or user-upload features. Public audio comes from independent churches and is not created or reviewed by the app developer. The app does not knowingly collect children's personal information for its own use.
